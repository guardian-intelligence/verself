# Design

Design is how it works but also how it makes you feel. These are some foundational philosophical thoughts on design. The user (Shovon Hasan / anveio ) is not a formally trained designer so please translate the notes here into relevant technical design terms.

## Letters

`https://guardianintelligence.org/letters`

These are letters from the founder for an audience of a single person. These are sometimes personal, vulnerable, and possibly even too much so. Thoughts on the industry, technology + short poems + memoirs on personal life.

The design is inspired by when I would write for hours every night to my partner who I had no way to contact while I waited for them. I wrote on coffee-stained graph paper by candlelight with my dog sleeping next to me. My handwriting was uneven and shifted between cursive and print, and they always started with the date prominently up top, sized to take up exactly two cell heights.
